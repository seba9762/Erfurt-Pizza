## ✅ Performance & Core Web Vitals

  - WebPageTest: https://www.webpagetest.org

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Keyword stuffing** - Use keywords naturally
2. **Duplicate content** - Each page should be unique
3. **Ignoring mobile** - 60%+ traffic is mobile
4. **Slow page speed** - Users abandon slow sites
5. **No local optimization** - Critical for restaurants
6. **Ignoring reviews** - Respond to ALL reviews
7. **Inconsistent NAP** - Keep Name/Address/Phone identical everywhere
8. **No schema markup** - Helps Google understand your site
9. **Not tracking results** - Use analytics to measure success
10. **Set and forget** - SEO requires ongoing work
